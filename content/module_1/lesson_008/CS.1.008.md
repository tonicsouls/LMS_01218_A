**[IMAGE 7: A digital maze where every exit is blocked by a red 'X']** On a mobile screen, the URL bar is often hidden or truncated. You can't hover. You can't easily audit the header. 

**[IMAGE 8: A large padlocked shield appearing over a smartphone]** The protocol for Mobile Signal Recovery: **Treat every unsolicited text as a Phish.** 

**[IMAGE 9: A person typing a official website address directly into a mobile browser]** Never click the link in the message. Open your browser. Type the official address yourself. Don't let your intimacy with the device become your undoing.
